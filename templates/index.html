Current criminal laws, procedure under BNSS 2023, crime-wise options and latest legal news

New criminal laws in force from 1 July 2024. Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) replaced the IPC, CrPC and Indian Evidence Act. Offences committed before that date stay under the old laws. Reference aid only, not legal advice. Always check the bare Act and current court rulings.
